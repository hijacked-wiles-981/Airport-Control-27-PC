## 🕹️ About

Airport Control 27 is a 3D air traffic control simulation from CCC Games, set across detailed international airports. Players manage ground and tower operations by coordinating pushbacks, taxi routes, runway movements, departures, and arrivals. Realistic procedures include SID and STAR routes, altitude and speed restrictions, runway limitations, radio communications, radar displays, and changing weather conditions. The game combines careful planning with fast decisions as traffic density, emergencies, and airport complexity increase.

Airport Control 27 is built for players who enjoy realistic simulations, aviation procedures, strategic multitasking, and high-pressure decision-making.

## ✨ Features

- 🚨 **Traffic Challenges** — Handle emergencies, taxiway conflicts, runway occupancy, and increasingly complex schedules.
- 🌧️ **Changing Conditions** — Respond to shifting winds, low visibility, rain, snow, and special flight situations.
- 🗺️ **Detailed Airports** — Operate across richly modeled airports with realistic markings, lighting, terminals, and airfield layouts.
- 🛫 **Air Traffic Control** — Direct departures, arrivals, taxiing aircraft, and runway operations in real time.
- 🚜 **Ground Operations** — Coordinate pushback positions, tugs, follow-me vehicles, taxi routes, and runway exits.
- 🛠️ **Custom Scenarios** — Create and share custom liveries and flight schedules through Steam Workshop support.
- 🖥️ **Flexible Displays** — Use surface movement radar, approach radar, flight progress strips, radio logs, 4K, and ultrawide layouts.
- 📡 **Authentic Procedures** — Use real-world approaches, departures, altitude limits, speed restrictions, and runway rules.
- 🎙️ **Radio Communications** — Issue instructions and manage synchronized pilot readbacks through realistic air-to-ground communications.
